Morning Brief Ultra Pro Max Tier

Morning Brief Ultra Pro Max Tier is a small personal program that reads its owner's own Google Calendar, Gmail and Google Tasks, and writes one daily summary page for that owner. It runs on the owner's own computer. It is read-only: it can view data but cannot send, change or delete anything.

What it asks Google for

  • Google Calendar: view events (calendar.readonly)
  • Gmail: view messages and settings (gmail.readonly)
  • Google Tasks: view tasks (tasks.readonly)

The data is processed on the owner's computer and is not sent to the developer or to any third party. See the privacy policy and terms of service.
