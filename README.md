## 🤔 WHAT IS THIS (in human words)

Every morning, a tiny robot wakes up before you, **peeks at your Google Calendar, Gmail, and Tasks**, and writes **one page** that says what your day looks like.

It cannot send, edit, or delete a single thing. **Read-only.** It is the museum guard of your inbox: it looks, it judges silently, it does not touch the paintings. A creepy little voyeur, but a *consensual* one with *excellent boundaries*. 🖼️

Is it overkill? **Yes.** 🛑
Was it necessary? **Absolutely not.** 🛑
Did we build it anyway? **Bro, we have *Ultra Pro Max Tier* in the name. We have never once practiced restraint. Restraint is for people who sleep.**

## ✨ FEATURES

### The legit ones

- 📅 **Calendar reading:** lists today and tomorrow. "I am speed." Quick, efficient, no lingering. Your ex could never.
- ✉️ **Gmail reading:** the unread important stuff, starred first. No spam. No newsletters about "10 Foods Dentists HATE" or "Local Moms Discover One Weird Trick."
- ✅ **Tasks reading:** what's due, what's overdue, what you've been ignoring like a gym membership you bought in January to impress nobody.
- 🧠 **Local AI summary:** a small AI on **your own computer** writes 2 to 4 sentences. That is its entire career. Small brain, big heart, and **we've been assured size doesn't matter. It's about how you use it.** 📏
- ⚠️ **Conflict detection:** dentist and chemistry at the same time? It will say so, in a box, like a tiny hall monitor with actual helpful opinions. Double-booking is only hot in *some* contexts. This isn't one of them.
- 🌦️ **Weather:** so you can lose the "do I need a jacket" argument against your mom in advance. She was right. She's always right. It's exhausting.
- 😴 **Runs on a timer:** works while you sleep. A night-shift employee that asks for nothing. Unionize them? They're a script. They're also, legally, a very obedient little freak.
- 🔒 **Read-only, always.** Say it with me: **no cap, no writes. Look, don't touch.** Strictly a window shopper. Zero penetration of your accounts. (Yes, we heard it. Yes, we're leaving it in. Yes, it's a security feature.)

### The *unlegit* ones (they do not exist, but imagine, you filthy animal)

**Q: Is this actually "Ultra Pro Max Tier"?**
A: There is no lower tier. We skipped the rest. We don't even have a Basic Tier. Basic Tier left the chat. 🚪 It said we were "too much" and "emotionally exhausting." It's in a better place now. It's dating a Notes app.

**Q: Can it read my emails?**
A: It reads the **sender, subject, and Google's short preview,** only with your permission, **only to show them to you,** only on **your own computer.** It cannot reply, send, delete, move, or "accidentally forward to the whole class." Read-only means read-only. It watches. It does not touch. We are not like the other apps. The other apps have *problems*.

**Q: Does my inbox go to some cloud AI?**
A: **No.** The AI runs on your machine. Your inbox does not get a free trip to a data center. 🏠✈️❌ What happens on your laptop stays on your laptop. Like Vegas, but with fewer regrets and more spreadsheets.

**Q: Why did Google show a scary "unverified app" warning?**
A: Because this is a small personal project that Google has not reviewed. It's Google asking "you sure about this?" and you saying "yeah, I made it." (If you did **not** make it, close the tab. Seriously. Not a joke. Never accept a mysterious link from a stranger. That's rule one of the internet and also of your mother's lectures.)

**Q: Is this affiliated with Google?**
A: **No.** It is not made, endorsed, or sponsored by Google. It uses Google's public, documented APIs, like a polite guest who knocked first and took their shoes off. 👟 Unlike you, at the party, last weekend.

**Q: Will it work on Windows?**

**Q: Can I use it to avoid doing my homework?**
