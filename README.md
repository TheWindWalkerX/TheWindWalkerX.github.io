## 🤔 WHAT IS THIS (in human words)

Every morning, a tiny robot wakes up before you, **peeks at your Google Calendar, Gmail, and Tasks**, and writes **one page** that says what your day looks like.

It cannot send, edit, or delete a single thing. **Read-only.** It is the museum guard of your inbox: it looks, it judges silently, it does not touch the paintings. 🖼️

Is it overkill? **Yes.** 🛑
Was it necessary? **Absolutely not.** 🛑
Did we build it anyway? **Bro, we have *Ultra Pro Max Tier* in the name. What did you expect?**

## ✨ FEATURES

### The legit ones

- 📅 **Calendar reading:** lists today and tomorrow. "I am speed."
- ✉️ **Gmail reading:** the unread important stuff, starred first. No spam. No newsletters about "10 Foods Dentists HATE."
- ✅ **Tasks reading:** what's due, what's overdue, what you've been ignoring like a gym membership.
- 🧠 **Local AI summary:** a small AI on **your own computer** writes 2 to 4 sentences. That is its entire career. Small brain, big heart.
- ⚠️ **Conflict detection:** dentist and chemistry at the same time? It will say so, in a box, like a tiny hall monitor with actual helpful opinions.
- 🌦️ **Weather:** so you can lose the "do I need a jacket" argument against your mom in advance.
- 😴 **Runs on a timer:** works while you sleep. A night-shift employee that asks for nothing. Unionize them? They're a script.
- 🔒 **Read-only, always.** Say it with me: **no cap, no writes.**

### The *unlegit* ones (they do not exist, but imagine)

- 🎤 **Roast Mode:** the AI reads your overdue tasks aloud like a disappointed judge on a cooking show. "It's raw. The homework is RAW."
- 🎺 **Dramatic Airhorn Mode:** every deadline gets a sound effect.
- 🕵️ **Detective Mode:** "The calendar has been *tampered with*, Watson. By Mondays."
- 🧦 **Dirty Laundry Mode:** displays your overdue tasks in a pile, smells included. *(Ew. Wash your tasks.)*
- 🐐 **GOAT Mode:** the page just says "you got this" in 72pt font and nothing else. Honestly more useful than half the apps on your phone.

## ❓ FREQUENTLY UNASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: Is this actually "Ultra Pro Max Tier"?**
A: There is no lower tier. We skipped the rest. We don't even have a Basic Tier. Basic Tier left the chat. 🚪

**Q: Can it read my emails?**
A: It reads the **sender, subject, and Google's short preview,** only with your permission, **only to show them to you,** only on **your own computer.** It cannot reply, send, delete, move, or "accidentally forward to the whole class." Read-only means read-only. We are not like the other apps.

**Q: Does my inbox go to some cloud AI?**
A: **No.** The AI runs on your machine. Your inbox does not get a free trip to a data center. 🏠✈️❌

**Q: Why did Google show a scary "unverified app" warning?**
A: Because this is a small personal project that Google has not reviewed. It's Google asking "you sure about this?" and you saying "yeah, I made it." (If you did **not** make it, close the tab. Seriously. Not a joke.)

**Q: Is this affiliated with Google?**
A: **No.** It is not made, endorsed, or sponsored by Google. It uses Google's public, documented APIs, like a polite guest who knocked first and took their shoes off. 👟

**Q: Will it work on Windows?**
A: It was built for Linux. Windows might work. "Might" is doing a lot of heavy lifting here, like a very optimistic forklift. 🏋️

**Q: Can I use it to avoid doing my homework?**
A: It tells you *what* your homework is. Doing it is still, tragically, your department.

**Q: Why is this README longer than the actual website?**
A: Because the website had to be boring and legal. This README is free. **FREE, I SAY.** 🕊️

**Q: Is it really read-only?**
A: Yes. It literally does not have permission to do anything else. It is the digital equivalent of a window shopper with no wallet. 🛍️

**Q: Why the name?**
A: Hello there. *(General Kenobi.)* It was either this or "Morning Brief 2". We chose violence. 🗡️
